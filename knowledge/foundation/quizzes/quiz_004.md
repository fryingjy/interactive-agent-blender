# Retrieval quiz 004 — five-day applied-judgment check

**Date:** 2026-08-16

**Conditions:** These are fresh scenario questions written five calendar days after quiz 003. No

## 1. A product board has six images, but five show neighboring variants. Why is image count not readiness?

Reference readiness depends on what each image can authoritatively prove about the exact target.
Variant images may explain a shared mechanism but can contradict target-specific proportions, seams,
materials, or controls. Record identity and property scope separately, then seek exact-target views
for every decision that would otherwise become invented geometry.

## 2. What should happen when the front view supports two different component constructions?

Keep both interpretations provisional and search for a view that exposes the disputed relationship,
often the top, side, rear, or an oblique. Do not let a plausible front silhouette authorize topology.
The chosen construction should cite the secondary-view depth or boundary evidence, while the rejected
alternative remains recorded.

## 3. Why can a machine-ready reference board still require a human gate?

A machine gate can verify provenance, view coverage, dimensions, and explicit uncertainty, but it
cannot establish that the interpretation actually reads like the object to an experienced observer.
When earlier automated passes accepted visibly wrong models, the correct response is to preserve a
review gate rather than self-authorize another build.

## 4. When is one connected edit-mode cage preferable to adding ring or panel primitives?

Use a connected cage when the features belong to one manufactured skin and require continuous
silhouette, curvature, or editable loop flow. Add geometry through inset, extrusion, loop cuts, or
profile changes. Separate objects remain appropriate for real assemblies, moving parts, independent
materials, or components with genuine physical boundaries.

## 5. Why is “bevel every sharp-looking edge” still an incomplete hard-surface rule?

Sharpness intent must be represented deliberately and inspected in context. A weighted or otherwise
limited Bevel should cover the full semantic edge set, but widths, segments, angle transitions,
modifier order, scale, and nearby topology determine the evaluated result. Unrecorded blanket ANGLE
behavior should trigger review instead of guessed intent.

## 6. What does Smooth by Angle contribute, and what can it not repair?

Smooth by Angle controls normal interpolation across geometric angles, reducing inappropriate
blanket smoothing while preserving selected hard transitions. It does not create missing bevels,
support loops, circular topology, or accurate form. A smooth shading result can conceal blockout
errors and must be reviewed against the evaluated surface and silhouette.

## 7. Why should high-poly and low-poly modifier stacks remain unapplied in the editable deliverable?

Live modifiers preserve authoring intent, allow width or subdivision changes, expose the production
construction, and let the user decide when destructive application is appropriate. Verification
should inspect both base cages and evaluated meshes. Export copies may evaluate modifiers, but the
saved source should retain independent editable high and low variants.

## 8. Why must high-poly and low-poly objects use separate collections and independent meshes?

Separate collections make variant ownership, visibility, baking, export, and cleanup unambiguous.
Independent mesh datablocks prevent a low-poly edit from silently modifying the high-poly source.
Merely duplicating an object with shared data or joining disconnected shells does not prove a
purpose-authored low topology or a safe production package.

## 9. What is the modeling reason for deliberate UV seams in a standard unwrap?

Seams specify where the surface is cut open and therefore control island connectivity. Their goal is
not simply to make Unwrap run; placement should manage distortion, visibility, material direction,
packing, and bake continuity. Automatic projection can be useful in context, but it does not replace
an authored cut graph when those relationships matter.

## 10. What proves that a tangent-space normal bake is usable downstream?

Evidence must include a valid low UV layout, correct high-to-low correspondence, successful selected-
to-active bake, a Non-Color normal image, and an actual shader or export binding. A fresh import
should confirm required normals, tangents, UVs, materials, axes, and scale; an image file alone proves
only that pixels were written.

## 11. Why does a video lesson need before, during, and after evidence around one episode?

The before frame establishes the starting state, the during frame shows the demonstrated operation,
and the after frame confirms its result. Speech or captions must align temporally with those visual
states. Without this sequence, a summary can attribute narration to the wrong action or claim a
result that occurred outside the reviewed interval.

## 12. What should happen when a Gemini video response identifies a different source than requested?

Reject the analysis as provenance-invalid even if its advice sounds plausible. Bind every analysis
to requested and returned source identity, duration, and timestamp evidence. A cross-video response
belongs in the retained failure record; it cannot promote a lesson or steer an asset merely because
the generic modeling language appears useful.

## 13. When should structured retrieval abstain instead of returning its nearest skill?

Abstain when the ticket lacks enough query or typed-context overlap to clear the calibrated relevance
threshold. Runtime success and version metadata may rank genuinely relevant candidates but must not
manufacture relevance. An unrelated skill with a few generic words such as surface or deformation is
more dangerous than an explicit no-match result.

## 14. Why must an out-of-band Blender edit trigger fingerprint reconciliation?

The planner's observation may no longer describe the scene after vertices move, transforms change,
or modifiers are edited externally. Layered fingerprints detect those conflicts before mutation.
The system should reject stale authority, re-probe state, and begin a new decision rather than append
an apparently valid command to an invalid scene revision.

## 15. What did the visually rejected automated passes prove about silhouette metrics?

They proved that thresholds can be satisfied while recognition, color, component structure, and
actual reference resemblance remain poor. Global masks may reward coarse occupied area and miss
semantic mismatch. Metrics should localize discrepancies and support comparison, but direct visual
review can overturn a technical or IoU pass without being treated as noise.

## 16. How should hidden underside construction be handled when references do not resolve it?

Record the uncertainty and avoid inventing production-significant detail. Model only what the brief
requires and what visible geometry constrains; seek a targeted underside source if the hidden form
affects silhouette, mechanics, topology, or delivery. Decorative guesses should not become
authoritative simply because they make the model look more complete.

## 17. Why should loop cuts be placed by function rather than distributed uniformly?

Loops exist to control silhouette, curvature, transitions, deformation, or local detail. Uniform
density wastes geometry on straight regions and can starve high-curvature zones, producing worse
face aspect ratios and harder editing. A low-sided circular cage can remain clean when axial loops
are concentrated where the profile actually changes.

## 18. What are the limits of using To Sphere or LoopTools Circle to repair a circular opening?

Circularization regularizes an appropriate selected loop in a plane, but it cannot infer the right
center, radius, orientation, vertex count, or surrounding flow. The operator may distort adjacent
forms or produce a visually circular boundary with poor continuation. Verify planarity, spacing,
support topology, and the evaluated silhouette afterward.

## 19. When should a difficult prop be abandoned in favor of a simpler benchmark?

Change level when the failure is dominated by unresolved interpretation or missing foundational
judgment rather than one repairable region. Retain the rejection and its diagnosis, then choose a
simple but structurally different object whose primary form can be independently reviewed. This is
curriculum control, not permission to relabel an easier task as the original success.

## 20. What remains unproven after this five-day retrieval pass?

The pass does not prove factual correctness, independent expert judgment, closed-book cognition,
week- or month-scale retention, or professional modeling autonomy. It strengthens evidence that the
system can restate newer mechanisms in fresh scenarios. Those mechanisms still need externally
reviewed use across unfamiliar assets with limited correction and preserved editable results.
