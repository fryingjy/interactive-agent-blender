# 2026 curriculum additions

This document records the genuinely new sources in the user-supplied general 3D-modeling curriculum
of 2026-08-15. Thirteen of its 26 unique video IDs were already represented in
`BLENDER_MODELING_CURRICULUM_V2.md`; the thirteen additions below extend the queue without replacing
existing completed studies.

These URLs are study sources, not an archive. Process each through `VIDEO_EXTRACTION_PROTOCOL.md`.
No video counts as learned until a supported principle survives a different-geometry transfer test
and improves a later reference-driven decision.

## New sources

1. `K7__BjW4UWE` — Blender 5.0 beginner workflow: primitives, curves/pipes, furniture, scene
   construction. Extract primitive-to-editable-form decisions and curve-vs-mesh choices.
2. `OAW81QZpvRY` — complete beginner guide: extrusion, scale, bevel, knife, simple object and SubD
   construction. Extract the transition from primitive to controlled editable form.
3. `LyPPgW9GpKo` — chair from loop cuts and extrusion. Focus on form decomposition and obtaining
   multiple connected forms from one edited base mesh where appropriate.
4. `2YIvX3SeJQA` — video reference backgrounds. Treat as reference-media setup, not evidence of
   reference interpretation skill.
5. `xHJzDpijPqg` / companion `CrCX7N_Q3XE` — robot from front/side references. Both are
   currently private in the available browser session; retain the desired shared-landmark and
   cross-view curriculum requirements, but do not claim an extraction or retry the sequence.
6. `46XJ6_V5PN0` — carabiner from a curved reference. Compare direct polygon construction against
   curve-based construction and conversion.
7. `HH7InXu7ZdU` — smoothing and hard edges. Separate geometry, normals, SubD control, edge radius,
   crease, support-loop, and bevel-weight problems.
8. `MD1QmdqXRfc` — topology fundamentals. Extract surface-context decisions about edge flow, poles,
   density, beveling, symmetry, SubD, and weighted normals.
9. `_bpsEd_5IW4` — clean hard-surface topology. High priority: compare flat, hidden, curved, and
   highlight-critical topology rather than applying a simplistic all-quads rule.
10. `seFDI4pqnOo` — Boolean topology and cleanup. Extract when a Boolean is appropriate, what cleanup
    the evaluated surface requires, and when rebuilding is cleaner than repair.
11. `2Gg5QY5h9pQ` — cylinder topology and shading. Learn radial density from scale, curvature, and
    output needs; do not memorize one segment count.
12. `dEGJeVnWZAA` — furniture and multi-component scene construction. Extract repeated-component,
    modifier-reuse, bevel-weight, scale, and object-organization decisions.
13. `592xMYzz2Rw` — large repeated structures. Extract arrays, reuse, hierarchy, and complexity
    management rather than stadium-specific steps.

## Priority routing

Study the reference-alignment, topology, cylinder, smoothing, and Boolean sources first because they
directly address current gaps in reference interpretation and connected hard-surface construction.
UV/material sources already present in v2 remain necessary, but geometry and decision quality stay
ahead of decorative detail. Sculpting remains deferred.
