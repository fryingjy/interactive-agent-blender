Update this roadmap when evidence changes; never reinterpret evidence to defend an old plan.

## North star

Build and prove a generalizable reference-driven Blender modeler that reconstructs unfamiliar
hard-surface props and weapons with convincing form, appropriate editable construction, and
evidence-based self-correction. Broader subject coverage follows demonstrated competence.

Reference evidence must determine major form. Preserve ambiguity, compare competing 3D
explanations, construct incrementally, inspect the actual cage and evaluated surface, and repair
or rebuild when wrong. Knowledge supports this path; it must not duplicate geometry authority.

## Completion contract

Require **three consecutive held-out props across at least two construction families**. Freeze
targets before modeling; target-specific code or post-freeze target research contaminates a result.
Each result must:

1. Have sufficient multiple-angle evidence for silhouette, depth, components, overlap and negative space.
2. Compare at least two plausible shape/representation hypotheses where ambiguity exists.
3. Match major form, proportions, components and negative spaces across relevant views, with no
   `REJECT_MAJOR_FORM` review.
4. Use connected editable topology for continuous form and separate objects for genuine assemblies.
5. Preserve live modifiers and separate high/low collections where the brief requires them.
6. Pass independent technical checks **and** visual review; neither substitutes for the other.
7. Demonstrate autonomous evidence-driven repair, rollback or representation change.
8. Deliver a reproducible `.blend`, comparisons, decision history, limitations and honest review.

A failure resets the consecutive-success count. Development exercises and human corrections are
not autonomous held-out successes. Test counts, all-quads, source counts, silhouette IoU and polished
renders alone are not evidence of professional construction or generalization.

## Current evidence and failure

The retained pipeline is `reference → evidence → components/correspondence → cameras → competing
shape hypotheses → fit → construction/compile → typed Blender → inspect → diagnose/refit/rebuild`.
Controlled tests establish fitted families, bounded shared-cage compilation, typed rollback,
persistent identities and evaluated-surface probes. They do not establish ordinary-photo mastery.

The [frozen B0 report](BOOTSTRAP_BASELINE.md) is the authoritative baseline summary. Four neutral
families expose false image holes, fragmented appearance labels, and unresolved pose/component/depth
evidence. The drawing-assisted housing envelope fits two families well but cannot distinguish them
at the unchanged selection margin. Neither fit establishes cavity, lid or usable production surfaces.
The separate mask intervention fixes two source-reviewed backed surfaces; a tiny-aperture
counterexample rejects universal size-based hole filling. **No reference-task Blender artifact from
these baseline/intervention cycles exists yet.** B0 and the bootstrap remain partial.

Useful construction evidence is separate and bounded:

- [Neutral blade](../knowledge/foundation/surface_construction_baseline.json): closed diamond cage;
  CPU/Blender raster agreement is approximate, not exact.
- [Crease experiment](../knowledge/foundation/selective_crease_experiment.json): edge intent controls
  shrinkage and end rounding within one family; no universal crease recipe.
- [Cross-family repair](../knowledge/foundation/feature_edge_transfer_experiment.json): a manifold
  stepped housing still overlapped under SubD; explicit shoulder constraints removed the observed
  intrusion. Evaluated overlap candidates localize inspection, not a clean-surface verdict.
- A prior trident proxy passed silhouette selection but had distorted interior surfaces. Virtual
  fitting caps must never count as authored Blender faces or production-cage evidence.
- [Connected shell transfer](../knowledge/foundation/connected_shell_transfer.json): cube inset and
  extrusion produced a 16-vertex connected cavity; transfer to a radial cage exposed a polygonal
  rim that shading could not fix. Bevel/SubD plus measured diameter correction reduced radial
  variation about 93.5% while retaining the floor and live modifiers. This is an agent-guided
  neutral reproduction/transfer, not a reference model or bootstrap promotion. Apply the lesson to
  the missing reference construction next rather than accumulating more generic drills.

## Immediate work: close the reference-to-construction gap

The user-requested simple-object practice now has a real supplier-drawing washer blockout,
competing ring/solid fits, typed corrections and an isolated live-modifier trial. See
[washer practice](../knowledge/foundation/washer_reference_practice.json). Evaluated dimensions
pass the study tolerance after shrinkage compensation; this is not an accepted benchmark asset.
The raw-cage critic rejected polygonal curvature and later returned invalid structured feedback.
Diagnostic modifier trials do not constitute reviewer acceptance or bootstrap promotion.

1. Keep frozen raw B0 outcomes unchanged. Label interventions separately; do not tune baseline
   thresholds or quietly replace failed masks.
2. Finish the washer's stage-aware cage/evaluated review, then transfer the demonstrated circular
   surface control to a simple flanged spacer before a low-component mechanical assembly. Use
   dimensioned reference evidence where available; do not repeat metadata-only cycles or claim
   ordinary-photo mastery from engineering drawings.
3. Compare plausible explanations and identify a discriminating observation. A tied fit stays
   ambiguous: do not loosen the margin or use fixture authorization on a real reference.
4. Once the existing development authorization contract is satisfied, produce and inspect an actual
   editable Blender blockout. Compare authored faces, evaluated surfaces and fixed-view Workbench
   renders, not just filled-volume CPU silhouettes. Test one highest-impact correction.
5. Complete reproduction, cross-family transfer and adaptive application in the curriculum's four
   tracks: reference-to-form, construction grammar, surface control and adaptive repair. Research
   only the failure obstructing the next demonstrated action.
6. Resume held-out modeling only when bootstrap and independent/human review requirements pass.

An infrastructure-only cycle must identify the exact failing operation it unblocks and test that
operation. Documentation cleanup is maintenance, not modeling-skill promotion. A model upgrade
is an experimental variable, not proof of capability; comparisons must freeze task/tools/criteria.

## Subsequent phases

- **P0 exit:** one unused real multiview reference reaches provenance, constrained evidence,
  competing hypotheses, fitted blockout, typed execution, comparison renders and localized
  correction or justified rejection. Image evidence must determine major form.
- **P1/P2:** broaden construction families and adaptive edit-mode/surface decisions through transfer.
- **P3:** progressive benchmarks, simple single-component and low-component assemblies before
  complex weapons; establish breadth before complexity.
- **P4:** brief-appropriate topology, UV/materials, high/low organization and export.
- **P5:** satisfy the completion contract without changing thresholds after seeing targets.

## Modeling and efficiency policy

- Choose methods from form and deliverable, not universal slogans. Start box-like forms as boxes;
  avoid prematurely rounding corners that must remain sharp.
- Use sufficient circular topology, often 12–16 segments at blockout scale, and inspect the result.
- Extrude and add loops to a shared cage for continuous features; do not imitate them with primitive
  piles. Preserve real assembly boundaries instead of forcing everything into one object.
- Choose crease, support loops, bevel, weighted normals, flat or angle-based smoothing from intended
  surface/highlight behavior. Never blanket-smooth to conceal inaccurate form.
- Keep modifiers live and inspect base and evaluated geometry. Prefer cheap Workbench diagnostics.
- Treat review renders/screenshots as temporary; retain concise lessons, limitations and final
  editable models rather than growing a permanent image archive. Preserve active source bindings.
- No HTML reference boards or unnecessary pre-model approval requests. Respect actual runtime gates;
  do not fabricate approval evidence or independent reviews.
- Install tools only for demonstrated unmet needs. Preserve working architecture and user assets.
