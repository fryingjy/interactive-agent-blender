Established before blockout

  • Four primary assemblies: faceted boiler, faceted collector with integrated spout, hinged lid, and separate swept handle.
  • The boiler/collector/lid are eight-sided cast forms—not smooth cylinders or decorative panel stacks.
  • The spout must grow from the collector; the handle aperture and free lower tip are identity-critical.
  • Official scale anchors: 15 × 8.5 × 15.5 cm envelope, 72 mm boiler base.
  • The boiler underside, revision-specific graphics, and hidden casting details remain deliberately unmodeled/reversible.
