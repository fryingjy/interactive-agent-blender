# Master Lock 140D — connected body construction correction

## Diagnosed failure

The previous body was technically an all-quad, 8-vertex cube cage, but it was only scaled and bevel-modified. That is a valid blockout, not an adequate editable construction for a continuous primary manufactured form with an observed front seam.

## Chosen construction

The accepted body remains one mesh. Two loop cuts run around the existing vertical quad ring, then their positions are moved to z = -7.2 mm and z = -6.8 mm. The single front quad between them is recessed 0.15 mm. No separate seam strip, cylinder, boolean cutter, or applied modifier is used.

## Rejected construction

A seven-cut version created the same visual line but reserved six unnecessary body bands. The wireframe made that density visible, so it was rejected before saving.

## Verification boundary

The accepted base cage has topology-changing decision evidence, and the saved stage reports both base and evaluated surface state. The exact rear/underside remains low-confidence because the retained references do not establish it. This correction does not turn the asset into a production-approved model.
