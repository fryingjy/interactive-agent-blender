# Held-out metal watering-can benchmark brief

This fourth unrelated held-out family is frozen before source download or neutral rendering. The candidate may use only isolated reference pixels and measurements derived from those renders; source topology is prohibited.

## Construction contract

- Main vessel: one connected 16-sided ring loft. Circumferential ribs and shoulder/base shaping must come from the same cage through added rings, not stacked cylinders.
- Spout: one connected 12-sided tapered skin from body attachment to rose connector.
- Handle: one continuous curve/path member with explicit attachment points.
- Separate objects are limited to physically assembled or independently replaceable parts.
- Intended hard transitions must receive a deliberate bevel strategy; smooth circular regions must not be over-segmented.

## Frozen gates

- Side silhouette IoU >= 0.76
- Front silhouette IoU >= 0.67
- Top silhouette IoU >= 0.66
- Mean three-view IoU >= 0.72
- Fresh-process topology, UV, modifier, transform, export, and engine-import checks must pass.
- Planner checkpoints and a delayed retrieval/retention check must be recorded.

Automated success is not equivalent to experienced professional acceptance.
