Here are 8 distinct modifiers demonstrated in the video with clear before-and-after visual transformations, along with their practical modeling applications:

---

### 1. **Bevel Modifier**
* **Timestamp:** `01:03`
* **Visible Demonstration:** 
  * **Before:** A blocky, mechanical hard-surface geometry with razor-sharp 90-degree and angled edges.
  * **After:** As parameters are adjusted, the sharp edges are chamfered and smoothed into rounded bevels with multiple segments, catching specular light highlights along the borders.
* **Problem Solved:** **Hard-surface edge softening.** In 3D modeling, perfectly sharp 0-radius edges look fake and CGI-like; the Bevel modifier adds realistic edge rounding and specular highlights non-destructively without manually cutting support loops.

---

### 2. **Boolean Modifier**
* **Timestamp:** `01:12`
* **Visible Demonstration:** 
  * **Before:** A separate cube and sphere overlapping in 3D space.
  * **After:** Demonstrates the three boolean modes:
    * *Union:* Merges both into a single solid shell.
    * *Intersect:* Retains only the shared overlapping volume.
    * *Difference:* Carves a smooth spherical hollow out of the cube.
* **Problem Solved:** **Volumetric cutting and merging.** Essential for mechanical modeling, architectural cutouts (doors/windows in walls), and carving complex geometric recesses.

---

### 3. **Decimate Modifier**
* **Timestamp:** `01:31`
* **Visible Demonstration:** 
  * **Before:** A high-density wireframe of the Suzanne monkey head with thousands of quad polygons.
  * **After:** Lowering the ratio drastically reduces the face count into a low-poly triangular/quad mesh while preserving the general silhouette and facial features.
* **Problem Solved:** **Mesh optimization and LOD (Level of Detail) creation.** Useful for converting dense digital sculpts or 3D scans into lightweight assets for real-time game engines or distant background rendering.

---

### 4. **Subdivision Surface Modifier**
* **Timestamp:** `02:21`
* **Visible Demonstration:** 
  * **Before:** A faceted, low-polygon mesh with sharp angular contours.
  * **After:** Splitting each polygon into smaller quads smoothly rounds out the entire surface into an organic shape while keeping the underlying low-poly cage directly editable in Edit Mode.
* **Problem Solved:** **Subdivision/organic character modeling.** Allows artists to model complex, smooth, organic organic curves easily using a minimal number of control vertices.

---

### 5. **Remesh Modifier**
* **Timestamp:** `02:47`
* **Visible Demonstration:** 
  * **Before:** An extruded 3D capital letter "B" with messy, uneven n-gons and stretched side topology.
  * **After:** The mesh surface is converted into uniform blocks/voxels and restructured into an evenly spaced, uniform grid of clean quads across the entire letter.
* **Problem Solved:** **Fixing dirty CAD/Text topology for sculpting.** Reconstructs meshes with chaotic or stretched geometry into uniform quads suitable for sculpting brushes and displacement.

---

### 6. **Skin Modifier**
* **Timestamp:** `03:12`
* **Visible Demonstration:** 
  * **Before:** A simple 1D stick-like sequence of connected edges and vertices (a wireframe skeleton).
  * **After:** Thick 3D quad tubes are generated around the skeletal lines; adjusting per-vertex radius changes local limb thickness, which produces an organic base mesh when paired with Subdivision Surface.
* **Problem Solved:** **Rapid creature/character base mesh blockouts.** Instead of extruding cylinders by hand, you can sketch stick figures with vertices and immediately get a sculptable 3D body volume.

---

### 7. **Curve Modifier**
* **Timestamp:** `04:33`
* **Visible Demonstration:** 
  * **Before:** A straight, rigid chain of repeating geometric blocks.
  * **After:** When assigned to a curved spline path, the straight mesh bends and follows the exact S-curve of the spline in 3D space.
* **Problem Solved:** **Conforming linear objects to paths.** Essential for creating cables, ropes, tank treads, train tracks, belts, and spine-like segmented objects that need to flow along arbitrary curves.

---

### 8. **Lattice Modifier**
* **Timestamp:** `05:15`
* **Visible Demonstration:** 
  * **Before:** A complex cylindrical cage mesh with hundreds of detailed window-like cutouts.
  * **After:** A low-resolution 3D bounding box (lattice) is placed around it; moving and scaling just a few lattice points seamlessly bends and tapers the dense underlying cage without manually moving thousands of individual vertices.
* **Problem Solved:** **High-level freeform deformation of dense geometry.** Allows global shaping, squashing, and bending of highly detailed objects without destroying their fine surface details.
