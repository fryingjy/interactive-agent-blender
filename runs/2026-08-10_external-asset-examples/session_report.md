# User-supplied asset example inventory

Seven diverse, non-firearm `.blend` files from the supplied library opened successfully in Blender
5.2. The sample spans a small armor scene, stylized props, a sports asset, and highly populated
watch/Poké Ball scenes. Modifier counts range from 2 to 459.

This is useful breadth and provenance evidence, but it is deliberately not promoted to professional
quality evidence. The files had no cameras, no visual review was performed, authorship was not
independently established, and an experienced-modeler acceptance is still required for professional
stages 4–7. See `asset_inventory.json` for the measured scene statistics.
