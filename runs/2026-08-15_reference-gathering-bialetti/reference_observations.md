# Bialetti Moka Express 3-cup — reference-only field notes

## Target identity

The target is the classic aluminum 3-cup/130 ml Moka Express geometry, anchored to Bialetti's
published 155 mm height and 72 mm boiler-base diameter. The same-object photo set is eBay item
`197253718978`: twelve photos of one physical, pre-owned, Made-in-Italy 3-cup unit with its box and
manual. It supplies consistent shape evidence but is not dimensional authority.

## Visual findings

- The shells are genuinely eight-sided cast forms, not cylinders with decorative panels.
- The boiler, collector, lid, handle, knob, valve, hinge and internal filter stack are legitimately
  separate manufactured parts. “One object” does not mean welding these real assemblies together.
- Within each shell, the form should come from one continuous cage: lower facets flow into the
  threaded neck; upper facets flow into shoulders, rim and integrated spout. Primitive stacking is
  not an acceptable substitute for those transitions.
- The collector is taller than the boiler. The joint is a shallow narrow waist, not a full third
  body section.
- The spout is an open tapered channel integrated into one collector sector. It is not a cone or
  tube attached to the wall.
- The handle's negative space and swept lower tip are identity-critical. Its rear view is narrow;
  its side view is broad and curved.
- The lid has a low eight-sided crown, small perimeter lip, center knob and rear hinge. Its underside
  has radial ribs visible only when open.
- The upper chamber is hollow and faceted, with a rounded floor leading into the central delivery
  column. A closed solid shell would be structurally false even if the outside silhouette matched.
- Edge character is cast/manufactured: facets should remain planar, design breaks should be crisp,
  and only physical edge radii should receive controlled beveling. Global smooth shading would erase
  the defining planes.

## Conflicts and unknowns

- Reported retailer heights range from 155 to 180 mm. The 155 mm Bialetti value wins; retailer
  values are not averaged.
- The same-object set does not show the boiler underside. No underside stamps or deep recesses may be
  invented. If modeling proceeds, keep that surface simple and reversible pending a direct source.
- The overall width/depth question is no longer unresolved: a targeted follow-up found Bialetti's
  official 3-cup envelope of
  `15 x 8.5 x 15.5 cm`. It controls overall envelope dimensions only; the decorative SKU does not
  authorize graphics, wear, or hidden construction.
- The listing's packaging appears older than current official photography. Geometry transfer across
  production revisions remains a medium-confidence assumption and must be checked during human board
  review.

## Question-driven follow-up

The reference-workflow lesson's question-first rule was applied rather than gathering more images
at random. Three questions generated six exact searches and seven inspected candidates. Two
manufacturer evidence links were accepted into the factual set; a conflicting retailer dimension, two
non-underside galleries, and vintage/revision-mismatched evidence were retained as rejected
candidates with reasons. The direct underside remains deliberately deferred under a reversible
modeling constraint instead of being silently invented.
