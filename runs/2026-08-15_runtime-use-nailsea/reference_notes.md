# Same-object multi-reference analysis

The replacement asset is one documented Nailsea hollow-stem glass candlestick, not a collage of lookalikes. Five downloaded listing photos remain under ignored `media/`; their roles are frozen in `experiment_contract.json`.

The complete elevation establishes the outer profile: broad circular foot, long rising lower cone, small cushion knop, large lower knop, a long concave waist into a broad shoulder, two smaller upper knops, and a flared hollow socket. Close views disambiguate the transitions that the full view compresses. The oblique and bottom views establish circular width/depth and a real hollow socket. Published dimensions are 30.5 cm high and 9 cm in both width and depth.

The extracted full-view mask measures 0.292857 width/height versus the published 0.295082 ratio, an absolute difference of about 0.00223. That agreement is an external check on the segmentation scale. The mask is centered from the detected total per-row span because transparent glass caused either side to disappear in isolated rows; it is still a derived modeling proxy and not raw ground truth.

The white pulled trails are surface decoration visible across all views. They should become material/texture work later, not extra cylinders or fake structural ridges in this primary-form runtime test.
