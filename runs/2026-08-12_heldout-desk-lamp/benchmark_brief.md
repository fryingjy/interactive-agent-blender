# Held-out articulated desk-lamp benchmark

The candidate is modeled only from neutral source renders after this document is written. It must
test component-graph and strategy judgment rather than forcing an inappropriate one-object rule.
Rigid, independently articulated arms, hubs, clamp, and shade may remain separate. Each arm shell
and each spring/cable must be an intentional continuous member rather than disconnected primitives.

Automated silhouette, topology, UV/export, named-engine, and planner gates are necessary evidence;
they do not establish professional human acceptance.
